# Phase 1 NASA data inventory

The study focuses on soybean in Argentina's Pampas. Use the provisional rectangle from 38°S to 30°S and 64°W to 57°W for exploratory acquisition. In west, south, east, north order the bounds are `-64,-38,-57,-30`. The rectangle is an acquisition window. Any regional inference needs a documented Pampas boundary or explicitly named subregions. The trend period is January 2006 through December 2025 inclusive. Retain UTC in the source data and document any later conversion to local civil days.

NASA Common Metadata Repository (CMR) collection and latest-granule metadata checked November 14, 2026. An open catalog end date indicates an ongoing collection. The latest granule provides evidence of a catalog entry at that date, while continuity, local QA, Earthdata authorization, and actual downloads require separate checks. ## Access, latency and interpretation

- **GLDAS.** Resolve the pinned CMR collection to an official granule link. GES DISC advertises direct authenticated HTTPS NetCDF-4 at `data.gesdisc.earthdata.nasa.gov` and granular OPeNDAP links at `opendap.earthdata.nasa.gov`. Direct HTTPS followed by a local cutout is the simplest route for one small monthly source granule. Check OPeNDAP variable and geographic subsetting before scaling. Main-stream availability depends on GPCP inputs with approximately 3–4 months of latency. Early products are a separate forcing stream with roughly 1.5 months of lag. Avoid mixing them. GLDAS is an open-loop land simulation forced by meteorological inputs, including downscaled GPCP precipitation. At 0.25° a cell represents regional conditions. Monthly and three-hourly products are two time representations of one simulation. [NASA monthly product](https://data.nasa.gov/dataset/gldas-noah-land-surface-model-l4-monthly-0-25-x-0-25-degree-v2-1-gldas-noah025-m-at-ges-di-23acd) · [GES DISC three-hourly product](https://disc.gsfc.nasa.gov/datasets/GLDAS_NOAH025_3H_2.1/summary?keywords=GLDAS) · [NASA GLDAS dataset notes](https://ldas.gsfc.nasa.gov/gldas/dataset)
- **IMERG.** Use the catalog's authenticated GES DISC HTTPS NetCDF route under `/data/GPM_L3/GPM_3IMERGDF.07/`. NASA documents about 3.5 months of Final Run latency after the observation month. Retrieval inputs and gauge adjustments affect interpretation. Restrict a V07B comparison to its verified end. October–December 2025 require a separately verified, consistent collection or exclusion from the comparison. Check V08 availability and its full historical coverage before selecting an alternate precipitation series. [GES DISC daily Final product](https://disc.gsfc.nasa.gov/datasets/GPM_3IMERGDF_07/summary?keywords=imerg) · [NASA V07B release notes](https://gpm.nasa.gov/resources/documents/imerg-v07-release-notes) · [NASA transition notice](https://gpm.nasa.gov/data/news/imerg-v08-transition-schedule) · [NASA latency documentation](https://gpm.nasa.gov/resources/faq/what-determines-latency-imerg)
- **MODIS.** Use Earthdata Login and the LP DAAC CMR HTTPS HDF-EOS links or authenticated AppEEARS area requests. Standard composite availability follows the observation window and processing. No stable release lag was verified. For issue-time use, obtain timestamped availability evidence and do not infer it from the composite's nominal date. Screen detailed QA and pixel reliability for cloud, aerosol, poor geometry and other contamination. Vegetation indices are sensitive to mixed land cover and crop phenology. [LP DAAC product catalog](https://water.usgs.gov/catalog/datasets/dd93cc96-a562-4966-9033-8b99be306eec/) · [Collection 6.1 VI guide](https://lpdaac.usgs.gov/documents/621/MOD13_User_Guide_V61.pdf) · [NASA AppEEARS](https://appeears.earthdatacloud.nasa.gov/)
- **SMAP.** Use Earthdata Login and CMR HTTPS HDF5 at `data.nsidc.earthdatacloud.nasa.gov`, or NSIDC's supported Harmony subsetting. NSIDC reports mean generation lags of about 1 day 6 hours for enhanced L3 and 2 days 16 hours for L4, with availability to be checked separately. L3 surface retrievals have overpass gaps, multidimensional flags and effective spatial support coarser than the 9 km posting. L4 assimilates SMAP brightness temperatures and uses IMERG V07 precipitation in regions including Argentina. Treat L4–IMERG associations as relationships with shared inputs. [Enhanced L3 V6](https://nsidc.org/data/spl3smp_e/versions/6) · [L4 V8](https://nsidc.org/data/spl4smgp/versions/8) · [NSIDC release latency](https://nsidc.org/data/user-resources/help-center/what-are-latencies-smap-radiometer-data-sets) · [NSIDC Harmony notice](https://nsidc.org/data/user-resources/data-announcements/user-notice-nasa-earthdata-harmony-data-transformation-services-now-available-smap-l2-l4-data-sets)

## Historical comparability

- GLDAS monthly is the candidate backbone for the 2006–2025 trend. CMR returned 240 entries, one for each expected month. The three-hourly query returned 58,440 hits, matching the expected eight slots per UTC day by count. Only the monthly query checked individual time keys. Neither query checked file delivery, grid consistency, or valid values.
- SMAP cannot cover 2006–2014. Compare from a common complete interval, such as January 2016 through December 2024 for an initial full-year comparison, after granule and local quality checks. Favor one fully reprocessed SMAP release per product, record each file's composite release or science version ID, and inspect any changes or outages before estimating trends. Enhanced L3 V5 and L4 V6 and V7 are retired. L4 V7's catalog span was March 2015 through July 3, 2025. V8 changed the precipitation forcing and assimilation inputs, so treat versions as distinct analyses. Enhanced L3 and L4 share the SMAP instrument and represent different algorithms and sampling. Compare matched surface depths and overpass times where practicable. [NSIDC version history](https://nsidc.org/data/smap/version-history) · [Enhanced L3 V5](https://nsidc.org/data/spl3smp_e/versions/5) · [L4 V6](https://nsidc.org/data/spl4smgp/versions/6) · [L4 V7](https://nsidc.org/data/spl4smgp/versions/7)
- GLDAS precipitation forcing is part of the process that drives simulated soil moisture. Its relationship with GLDAS moisture cannot establish independent observational confirmation. For multiproduct comparisons, record shared inputs and processing, use common spatial support, and report usable observations for each paired time window.
