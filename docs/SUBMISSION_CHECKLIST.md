# NASA submission checklist

## At kickoff

- [ ] Confirm team registration, virtual event and correct challenge.
- [ ] Review official Details, Resources, judging criteria, required fields, asset limits and applicable portal deadline.
- [ ] Confirm the eligible NASA products, licenses, redistribution constraints and citation requirements.

## Scientific evidence and demo

- [ ] Document region, crop, dates, product versions, units, QA, missingness, spatial support and latency.
- [ ] Reproduce the historical analysis and verify trend magnitude, uncertainty, significance treatment, geographic comparison and cross-variable interpretation.
- [ ] Document the prediction target, chronological split, baseline results, issue-time assumptions and whether prediction or replay display is supported. Check that claims concern environmental conditions.
- [ ] Verify real data display, labels, missing and stale states, mobile usability, and the public demo link or permitted fallback.
- [ ] Verify fresh setup, release commit, reproducibility instructions, citations and credits. Inspect files and outputs for credentials and private field information.

## Portal delivery

- [ ] Prepare the required narrative, media, links and attribution using the official instructions confirmed at kickoff.
- [ ] Verify the judging audience can open each submitted link, including any required repository or alternative access. Ask for approval before changing repository visibility.
- [ ] Submit before the applicable deadline, then verify submitted status and preserve the submission URL and confirmation.
