# Phase 7 agricultural intelligence

Generic indicators use the latest released archived GLDAS storage at issue time and normals fitted on past training data. The dashboard shows the cell anomaly in mm, its regional area-weighted comparator, whether an anomaly is below normal, and the consecutive count of below-normal months. The count checks at most 12 months, stops at a missing month, masked value or nonnegative anomaly, and remains unavailable when the latest value is missing. These indicators describe modeled soil-water conditions rather than crop stress.

The experimental explorer also shows the last observation month and the assumed lag, the uncalibrated low-moisture fraction for a selected horizon, and pooled interval metadata for continuous outcomes. The month-end timestamp refers to the historical hindcast issue. It does not assert that the observation was genuinely published on the inferred availability date.

Soybean, maize and wheat use the same generic indicators. Crop-specific thresholds, stage guidance and recommendations remain research pending. INTA guidance considers environment and maturity group for soybean, sowing-window differences for maize, and seasonal conditions for wheat. Those local examples do not establish one uniform calendar for the entire provisional Pampas box. [INTA soybean environments and maturity groups](https://www.argentina.gob.ar/noticias/cultivo-de-soja-fecha-de-siembra-y-grupo-de-madurez-para-potenciar-el-rendimiento) · [INTA maize regional windows](https://www.argentina.gob.ar/noticias/siembra-de-maiz-que-hacer-en-cada-region) · [INTA seasonal context for wheat](https://www.argentina.gob.ar/noticias/la-variabilidad-del-clima-un-desafio-de-adaptacion-campana-campana-0)

Reproduce the indicators with the [spatial CLI](PHASE7_SPATIAL_FORECASTING.md#reproduce-and-display). Deterministic tests cover missing values and interrupted dry runs. Any future crop inference should link documented local calendars and crop stage to independently validated field or profile data and assess differences by crop and subregion before displaying decision advice.
