# Project vision

Quant Alpha Agro will help Argentine agricultural producers understand how water-related conditions have changed across seasons and locations and what conditions merit monitoring. A farmer should be able to select a supported field location on a phone or computer and see the environmental evidence at the source grid's scale.

The project has three planned parts. Historical scientific analysis will quantify trend magnitude, uncertainty, significance, geographic variation, and relationships. Hydrological risk assessment will evaluate whether an explicit environmental outcome can be predicted with useful historical skill. The web interface will explain the observations and any validated prediction in plain language.

Soybean, maize, and wheat are the intended crops. The first demonstration will cover one Argentine region and one crop selected after source verification. Planting date, crop stage, and 7-, 14-, or 28-day horizons are candidates for later expansion. Any crop-specific interpretation needs separate supporting evidence. The initial result will describe environmental conditions, with observation dates and the effective spatial footprint visible.
