# Second-phase analysis design

These sensitivity rules were recorded before running the second-phase analyses, after the full-record result was already known. Treat intervals as pointwise unless stated otherwise.

- Compare the full period, two fixed 10-year halves (2006–2015, 2016–2025), and the centered 15-year window (2009–2023). Use calendar-month fixed effects and 12 calendar-month HAC lags; check 6 and 24 lags on the full period.
- Compare spherical-area and equal-cell weighting, an interior with four 0.25° cells trimmed from each edge, and coverage thresholds of 80%, 95%, and 98%. Withhold estimates where a threshold excludes every month. Remove the 12 largest absolute calendar-month anomalies in one influence check; retain calendar gaps in HAC.
- Compare seasonal Sen slope using a seeded circular moving-block bootstrap of residuals from its own month-of-year-plus-trend fit. Resample aligned 24-month blocks (1,999 replicates; 95% percentile interval). Treat this interval as approximate under weak dependence and stable residual behavior.
- Split the box at 34°S and 60.5°W. Compare quadrant slopes and the paired North-minus-South and West-minus-East trends by calendar month. Report cell quantiles and the largest edge-connected patches of same-sign slopes (no significance claim based on connectivity).
- Describe seasonal means, annual departures, the three largest and smallest monthly departures, and anomaly correlations at 1, 3, 6 and 12 months. Use paired 24-month moving-block resampling for correlation intervals, with true chronological pairs computed before resampling. Compare the standard deviation of seasonally adjusted and detrended residuals between the fixed halves, with separate block resamples in each half. Ten years per half gives limited information about variability change.
- Inventory monthly data for precipitation, evapotranspiration, temperature and runoff. Require validated, aligned complete monthly series on common support before estimating retrospective relationships. Use detrended calendar-month anomalies, fixed lags (0, 1 and 3 months), HAC uncertainty, and one Benjamini–Hochberg adjustment over all tested predictors and lags.
